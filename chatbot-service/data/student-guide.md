# Student guide

## Getting your certificate

You don't request a certificate directly — your institution issues it to you once
they've generated it through AuthNode. You don't need to do anything until it exists.

## Finding your certificates

1. Sign in choosing the **Student** role.
2. Enter your name **exactly as your institution entered it** when issuing the
   certificate — matching is case-insensitive, but the name itself needs to match.
3. Open the **Dashboard**. Every certificate issued to that name appears there, each
   with its own QR code and Certificate ID.

## What is a Certificate ID?

It's a short code (the first 12 characters of your certificate's cryptographic
fingerprint) that uniquely identifies your certificate. Anyone with this ID — or the
QR code that encodes it — can verify your certificate is genuine without needing an
account.

## Sharing your certificate with an employer

From your Dashboard, you can share either:
- The **QR code** — the employer scans it and lands directly on the verification
  result, or
- The **verification link / Certificate ID** — share it as text and the employer
  pastes it into the Verify page themselves.

Either way, no login is required on their end.

## What if my certificate doesn't verify?

If a certificate you know is genuine shows "Not found" or "Does not match," see the
troubleshooting guide — common causes are a mistyped ID or an outdated/copied QR link.
If the details on your certificate were changed after issuance, verification will
correctly fail, since that's exactly what it's designed to catch.
